## English

### 1. Parties and scope

This draft agreement is between the contributor identified through the future acceptance record
(“Contributor”) and Darkadia Studio, whose complete legal identity and contact details must be added
before activation (“Project Operator”).

It covers only contributions expressly linked to the accepted CLA version and submitted to the
official Compendlore project repository in which this file appears, including code, documentation,
tests, designs, and other copyrightable material intentionally submitted for inclusion
(“Contribution”).

It does not automatically cover independent addons published on Compendlore Hub, private Compendlore
repositories, internal or professional work governed by another agreement, or unrelated future
works.

### 2. Ownership and license grant

The Contributor retains all rights they hold in the Contribution. The Contributor does not assign
exclusive ownership to the Project Operator.

For the legal duration of the relevant rights, the Contributor grants the Project Operator a
non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free, and, to the extent permitted by law, irrevocable license to:

- reproduce, represent, display, perform, and communicate the Contribution;
- use, run, correct, modify, translate, adapt, and create derivative works from it;
- integrate it into, or remove it from, Compendlore CMS, official addons, the addon SDK, developer
  tools, official distributions, documentation, services, and other components directly associated
  with the Compendlore ecosystem;
- distribute, make available, operate, commercialize, and otherwise exploit those covered
  destinations;
- grant sublicenses and relicense the Contribution as part of those covered destinations.

This grant provides the flexibility needed to operate and license Compendlore; it is not a decision
to make Compendlore CMS paid. The CMS remains free and is intended to remain free. No right is
granted beyond rights the Contributor actually controls.

Mandatory moral rights, legally protected remedies, and rights that cannot be waived or licensed
remain unaffected. Where the law permits, the Contributor agrees not to assert moral rights solely
to prevent authorized exercise of the license, without authorizing false attribution or unlawful
treatment.

### 3. Contributor statements

The Contributor states, to the best of their knowledge, that:

- they authored the Contribution or have sufficient permission from every relevant rights holder;
- any required employer, client, co-author, or institutional authorization has been obtained;
- third-party material is clearly identified with its source, license, and applicable restrictions;
- the Contribution does not knowingly disclose confidential information, trade secrets, credentials,
  tokens, private keys, real personal data, or material obtained unlawfully;
- known patent, contractual, export, confidentiality, or other restrictions are disclosed to the
  Project Operator;
- the stated provenance of the Contribution is accurate.

Use of generative or other AI assistance must be disclosed when required by project policy,
including the tool and the Contributor’s review process. This CLA does not guarantee that rights are
available in AI-assisted output. The Contributor remains responsible for verifying provenance,
permissions, confidentiality, and compatibility.

These statements do not create rights in third-party material and do not override mandatory law.

### 4. Review, integration, and removal

The Project Operator may accept, reject, edit, adapt, revert, or remove a Contribution. Acceptance
does not create a duty to retain or maintain it.

A lawfully accepted Contribution may remain integrated after the Contributor leaves the community,
is suspended, or is excluded. Departure, suspension, or exclusion alone does not revoke the rights
already granted or require removal of the Contribution. This does not authorize retention of
unlawful content and remains subject to third-party rights, legal obligations, court orders, and
legally protected remedies.

### 5. Voluntary contribution and recognition

A voluntary community Contribution is made without compensation, royalties, or other payment unless
a separate written agreement states otherwise. This clause does not displace obligations under an
applicable employment, contractor, or services agreement.

Accepted Contributors are included by default in a public recognition list under their GitHub handle
or chosen name after they are informed. A Contributor may decline listing or later request that the
displayed name be changed or removed. Removal from the list does not require erasure from Git
history, archives, mirrors, or forks where erasure is not reasonably possible or legally required,
and does not revoke rights already granted in the Contribution.

### 6. Conduct, moderation, and security

Community participation remains subject to the code of conduct and security rules. Proportionate
measures may include a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion for rule violations,
malicious conduct, or security harm.

A temporary preventive restriction may also be applied when an account may be compromised. It is a
security measure rather than a disciplinary finding and should be reassessed when reasonably
possible. Measures remain subject to mandatory law and appropriate review.

### 7. Future acceptance record

If activated after legal review, the acceptance mechanism must retain at least:

- the signatory’s identity and, where applicable, the represented entity and authority;
- the exact CLA version;
- the acceptance date;
- the covered repository or other defined scope;
- the contributions linked to that acceptance.

Activation must not infer acceptance from old contributions. Material scope changes require a new
version and an appropriate acceptance process. No signature bot, check, or enforcement mechanism is
enabled by this draft.

### 8. No warranty and governing terms

The Contributor provides the Contribution without contractual warranty except for statements that
cannot lawfully be excluded. Applicable law, jurisdiction, notices, the Project Operator’s complete
identity, and contact details remain to be completed and legally validated before activation.

### 4. Revue, intégration et retrait

L’Exploitant du projet peut accepter, refuser, modifier, adapter, annuler ou retirer une
Contribution. Son acceptation ne crée aucune obligation de la conserver ou de la maintenir.

Une Contribution licitement acceptée peut rester intégrée après le départ, la suspension ou
l’exclusion du Contributeur. Le départ, la suspension ou l’exclusion ne révoque pas, à lui seul, les
droits déjà concédés et n’impose pas le retrait de la Contribution. Cette règle n’autorise pas le
maintien d’un contenu illicite et reste soumise aux droits des tiers, aux obligations légales, aux
décisions de justice et aux recours légalement protégés.

### 5. Contribution volontaire et reconnaissance

Une Contribution communautaire volontaire ne donne lieu à aucune rémunération, redevance ou autre
paiement, sauf accord écrit distinct. Cette clause n’écarte aucune obligation résultant d’un contrat
de travail, de prestation ou de service applicable.

Les Contributeurs acceptés sont ajoutés par défaut à une liste publique de reconnaissance sous leur
pseudonyme GitHub ou leur nom choisi, après en avoir été informés. Un Contributeur peut refuser
cette publication ou demander ensuite la modification ou le retrait du nom affiché. Le retrait de la
liste n’impose pas l’effacement des historiques Git, archives, miroirs ou forks lorsque cet
effacement n’est ni raisonnablement possible ni légalement requis, et ne révoque pas les droits déjà
concédés sur la Contribution.

### 6. Conduite, modération et sécurité

La participation communautaire reste soumise au code de conduite et aux règles de sécurité. Des
mesures proportionnées peuvent comprendre un avertissement, une restriction temporaire ou une
exclusion définitive en cas de violation des règles, de comportement malveillant ou d’atteinte à la
sécurité.

Une restriction préventive temporaire peut aussi être appliquée lorsqu’un compte paraît compromis.
Elle constitue une mesure de sécurité et non une sanction disciplinaire, et doit être réévaluée
lorsque cela est raisonnablement possible. Les mesures restent soumises aux règles impératives et à
une revue appropriée.

### 7. Futur enregistrement de l’acceptation

S’il est activé après validation juridique, le mécanisme d’acceptation doit conserver au minimum :

- l’identité du signataire et, le cas échéant, l’entité représentée et son habilitation ;
- la version exacte du CLA ;
- la date d’acceptation ;
- le dépôt couvert ou tout autre périmètre défini ;
- les contributions rattachées à cette acceptation.

L’activation ne doit pas déduire une acceptation à partir d’anciennes contributions. Une
modification substantielle du périmètre exige une nouvelle version et un processus d’acceptation
approprié. Aucun robot de signature, contrôle ou mécanisme d’application n’est activé par ce projet.

### 8. Absence de garantie et stipulations applicables

Le Contributeur fournit la Contribution sans garantie contractuelle, sauf pour les déclarations qui
ne peuvent légalement être exclues. Le droit applicable, la juridiction, les notifications,
l’identité complète de l’Exploitant du projet et ses coordonnées restent à compléter et à valider
juridiquement avant activation.

En cas de divergence entre les versions linguistiques, la version française prévaut, sous réserve
des règles impératives applicables.
